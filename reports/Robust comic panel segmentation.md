## Recover source detail before asking models again

The historical V28 failure is informative. V28 correctly found the difficult page 57's thirteen curved regions, but crop checks still rejected targets 8 and 10 after three detector tiers, two model repairs and one pixel correction. V5's padded controls passed; actual V29 then stopped at its 96-call ceiling without publication. V30 accepted all thirteen scenes after two geometry repairs, with no omission found in personal review and all 14 overview/panel steps passing a phone-sized Chrome test. A confirmed target-10 false alarm caused extra expansion; a separate target-4 warning has not been adjudicated as false. These results do not establish isolated scene masks, physical-phone behavior or cold cost. **The historical V28 qualified sequential prefix is pages 1–56**; separate V30 source-57 success does not establish a common-policy prefix 1–57. An older policy reached personal review of page 150, and page 151 has never been dispatched. A blanket component expansion altered 56 of 150 pages and 201 boxes, so it is unsuitable as an unqualified default. These are development results from this project, not published benchmarks. ([Sequential evidence](../specs/research/full-pdf-sequential-review-20261008.md), [actual V30 evidence](../specs/research/actual-source57-v30-validation-20261009.md))

Start by determining what information our 150-DPI JPEG analysis pages have lost. Inspect the PDF's embedded raster dimensions, masks, placement transforms and vector/text overlays. Compare representative difficult regions with native extraction or a lossless higher-resolution composite. Extracting one embedded image is correct only when it actually represents the displayed page; PDF composition can require several objects and clipping. Rendering an already low-resolution raster at 300 DPI does not create new evidence. PyMuPDF documents both extraction and clipped high-resolution rendering, making it a useful research reference rather than a reason to replace the existing renderer. ([Official image recipes](https://pymupdf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/recipes-images.html))

The proposed preprocessing policy is deliberately conditional. Maintain an authoritative readable page, record every transform, and derive alternate views for specific jobs. Full-page context establishes inventory and order; medium-resolution masks propose structure; native-resolution neighborhoods resolve uncertain edges. Compatible archive/folder image bytes remain intact. PDF output uses a documented authoritative composite while preserving the input PDF separately. All accepted geometry maps back to source pixels; output crops never come from cleaned, thresholded or hallucinated images.

| Analysis operation | Why test it | Failure to guard against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native-source audit and selective high-resolution rendering | Recover genuine thin lines, pale tips and lettering; spend pixels only where needed | Upsampling existing JPEGs without new information; extracting images while losing PDF overlays |
| Color and luminance background estimates | Handle yellowed paper, colored gutters and black backgrounds | Assuming page borders are blank or every page has white gutters |
| Adaptive thresholds and a small threshold ensemble | Compare stable foreground support under uneven illumination | Grayscale/binarization erasing faint colored artwork |
| Light edge-preserving denoise or local contrast | Improve proposal masks on noisy scans | Removing thin tips, exaggerating JPEG halos or inventing apparent boundaries |
| Conditional deskew | Improve line/gutter detection when multiple structural cues agree | Mistaking intentionally diagonal panels for scan rotation |
| Multiscale local regions | Reduce model pixels and graph-segmentation memory | Losing full-page ownership/context or cropping outside the refinement search area |

These are available techniques, **not measured improvements on our book yet**. OpenCV supplies adaptive thresholding, bilateral filtering and CLAHE; scikit-image supplies Sauvola/Niblack masks. Morphological opening removes foreground structures and closing connects regions, so kernel choice can change the answer. Preserve raw color as independent evidence, and test each transform against unchanged inputs. No generative restoration, gutter inpainting or permanent cleanup belongs in the authoritative page. ([Thresholding](https://docs.opencv.org/4.12.0/d7/d4d/tutorial_py_thresholding.html), [filtering](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d4/d13/tutorial_py_filtering.html), [CLAHE](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d5/daf/tutorial_py_histogram_equalization.html), [Sauvola/Niblack](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/auto_examples/segmentation/plot_niblack_sauvola.html), [morphology](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d9/d61/tutorial_py_morphological_ops.html))

## Classical algorithms offer useful geometry, not scene understanding

Deterministic methods deserve another role even though the original heuristic failed. Uniform-gutter recursive cuts cheaply describe regular grids; edge/Hough-line methods find drawn frames; background flood-fill and connected components recover irregular support. Each supplies different evidence. The research history also identifies their structural limits: recursive layouts struggle with nesting, flood-fill leaks through open borders, and quadrilateral recovery cannot express concave ribbons. A connected component can be a panel fragment, several touching panels, or a protruding character rather than a whole narrative scene.

The completed **automatically seeded local-segmentation** spike expanded the search neighborhood and supplied panel/rival/background seeds to watershed and mask-initialized GrabCut. It confirms the upstream difficulty: source connectivity does not supply reliable semantic seeds. Random walker remains a technique lead, not a measured improvement or an automatic next trial. GrabCut's rectangle mode labels everything outside that rectangle as background, so reusing an already clipped box as a hard boundary would additionally prevent recovery of the missing tip. ([Measured spike](../specs/research/seeded-boundary-spike-20261009.md), [watershed](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d3/db4/tutorial_py_watershed.html), [GrabCut](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d8/d83/tutorial_py_grabcut.html), [random walker](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/auto_examples/segmentation/plot_random_walker_segmentation.html))

Use stable integer source coordinates internally. On our 1128×1648 images, one unit of a 0–1000 coordinate grid spans roughly 1.13×1.65 pixels. Repeated normalization and rounding can matter at exactly the narrow boundaries under dispute. Map transforms once, round minimum edges outward/down and maximum edges outward/up according to the crop API's coordinate convention, and test actual pixel extents. A modest uncertainty margin is sensible; a large arbitrary expansion that swallows neighboring scenes is not a successful refinement.

## Separate scene identity, pixel ownership and reading windows

The proposed architecture has three distinct outputs for each region: **which narrative panel it is, which visible content belongs with it, and the rectangular viewport that lets a reader see that content**. A frame mask alone cannot determine shared-balloon ownership. A panel can contain disconnected artwork or white interiors, while a foreground figure crosses its frame. Keep visible frame masks, required-content associations, uncertain boundary bands and final viewports separate; allow overlap and multiple components. Content-aware panel research explicitly investigates extended regions beyond nominal frames, while MangaSeg supplies over 700,000 masks across 10,130 double-page images. Those labels create opportunity but their annotation conventions still need inspection. ([Content-aware research abstract](https://ken.ieice.org/ken/paper/20220311UC8r/eng/), [MangaSeg CVPR 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Xie_Advancing_Manga_Analysis_Comprehensive_Segmentation_Annotations_for_the_Manga109_Dataset_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf))

The proposed automatic pipeline imports and fingerprints source pages, computes cheap structural proposals, and obtains semantic inventory/order from the existing Sol baseline. A mask specialist may add frame/balloon/text evidence **after qualification; none screened here qualifies yet**. Source-resolution local refinement would build conservative viewports, with disagreement routing a localized question about missing content or ownership. The automatic-point trial shows that providing stronger semantic hints can separate major joins without preserving thin tips or even every supplied positive point. Require both semantic association and source-boundary completeness; do not use mask agreement, model score or seed compliance as proof. Once calibrated, clear regular layouts could bypass paid inference, but current agreement does not justify that shortcut.

Quality checks should present full-page context plus clean enlarged local evidence and the actual crop; an optional marked copy must draw the **exact integer crop boundary**. Our own marked-detail controls support investigating this representation, not certifying it universally. Independent VQA research also finds benefits from zoomed panel inputs, but those scores concern understanding rather than pixel completeness. Cache judgments by every input actually sent, including contextual metadata; unchanged crop bytes do not permit reuse when other judgment inputs changed. Repeatedly accepting and rejecting equivalent evidence indicates judge instability, not a reason to retry until acceptance. ([Zooming into Comics](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.06490v1))

Reading order needs a separate graph of before/after constraints, honoring explicit direction and credible metadata. Clear rows, columns and insets yield geometric constraints; spirals, interleaved strips and borderless montages require semantic resolution. Magi uses graph/topological ordering, and CHROMIC studies chronology across Western/Japanese comics, but its curated evaluation does not prove arbitrary-page ordering. Never blindly sort returned boxes or mirror pixels to implement RTL. Preserve alternate defensible orders in evaluation and abstain on unresolved cases. ([Magi project](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/research/magi/), [CHROMIC](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.205/))

The reader remains simple: complete page first, then ordered intact crops. Internal masks help detection and validation; initially they do not erase surrounding artwork for display. Unresolved pages remain explicit whole-page fallbacks, counted as unsegmented. The existing input/ACBF/reader work remains useful, and the proposed Rust boundaries should accept these richer records behind replaceable adapters. Rust production implementation stays deferred until quality and architecture discussion; porting the current cascade would preserve its weaknesses. ([Existing integration research](../specs/research/existing-comic-conversion-solutions.md), [Rust discussion plan](../specs/plans/0002-quality-gated-rust-cli.md))

## Measure complete pages across books before scaling spending

**Exact-page success is the product metric:** every required scene present, no harmful merge/split, complete associated art/text, and an allowed reading order. Box AP and mask IoU remain comparison diagnostics. Narrow missing stems or lettering can barely change IoU yet ruin a crop; Boundary IoU detects boundary errors more sensitively, but still does not decide semantic ownership. Measure required-content recall, maximum inward clipping, thin-structure loss, unrelated-scene intrusion, fallback rate and harmful false acceptance separately. A full-page viewport repeated for every panel must fail usefulness/readability criteria despite perfect recall. Freeze an annotation tolerance that distinguishes scan halos from meaningful artwork. ([Boundary IoU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.16562), [metric limitations](https://metrics-reloaded.dkfz.de/metric-library/boundary_intersection_over_union_local))

Use the present anthology for development and regression, including correct examples alongside pages 18/21/40/57/150. Freeze untouched **books and artists**, not adjacent pages, for final evaluation. Include Western color comics, B/W manga and RTL, 4-koma spreads, webtoons, borderless art, insets, shared balloons, curves, full bleed, aged/skewed scans and long pages. CoMix supplies the strongest directly relevant multi-domain benchmark framework found. eBDtheque contributes 100 diverse pages and order metadata; Manga109/MangaSeg provide dense manga supervision; DCM/COMICS add Western material, but machine-extracted crops are not independent gold labels. Verify data rights and annotation origin before use. ([CoMix paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03550), [framework](https://github.com/emanuelevivoli/CoMix), [eBDtheque](https://ebdtheque.univ-lr.fr/database/), [Manga109](https://manga109.github.io/manga109-project-website/en/), [MangaSeg terms](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MS92/MangaSegmentation), [COMICS](https://github.com/miyyer/comics))

The [acquired independent corpus](../specs/research/independent-fixtures-20261009.md) contains three xkcd strips, six original RTL Ubunchu pages and three tall Homem-Grilo sections, with [source-only inventories frozen before inference](../specs/research/independent-source-audit-20261009.md). It does not fill every artist, 4-koma, aged-scan or borderless-book coverage gap. Preserve attribution, original byte hashes, artist/book grouping and review annotations when scoring outputs. These sources are independent of our tuning anthology; absence from model pretraining is unknown. No qualification on these fixtures is claimed yet.

Score file-container support independently by passing equivalent pixels through PDF, CBZ, real CBR and folders. Add coordinate-transform and benign-resize/compression stability checks. Human review establishes development/test labels and assesses actual output; **it never supplies runtime geometry, seeds, branch selection or approvals**. Model confidence becomes a useful decision score only after calibration on unseen books. Statistical risk control offers a framework under explicit assumptions, not a promise of perfection after distribution shift. ([Conformal Risk Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814))
