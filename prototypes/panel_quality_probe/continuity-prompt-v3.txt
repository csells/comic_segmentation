Assess the main artwork in this unannotated comic region. Ignore tiny fragments of neighboring panels cut off along the outer image edge. Is the main artwork ONE continuous camera view, rather than multiple distinct narrative panels?

Trace perspective, object scale and physical connections across any apparent division. Architecture, furniture, shadows and foreground objects within the same camera view do not divide it into panels. Conversely, an authored panel border or gutter may be diagonal, curved, black, white or colored. It need not form a rectangular grid. A change of viewpoint, framing or scale across a drawn divider establishes separate panels even when their backgrounds and characters match. A close-up beside a wider view is not one continuous camera view merely because both depict the same moment. Judge visual continuity, not story continuity.
