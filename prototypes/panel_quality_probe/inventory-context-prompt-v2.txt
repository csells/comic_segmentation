Examine the original complete comic page, then the enlarged unannotated region from it. Determine whether that region depicts one continuous composition or multiple distinct views. Use the complete page as context. A wall edge, doorway, foreground subject, or change in scale from ordinary perspective is not a panel boundary. A view is an independently presented composition: a framed or bordered panel, an inset with its own boundary, a panel whose boundary is partly hidden by another panel, or a borderless composition separated from its neighbors by a clear gutter or spatial break. One continuous composition stays one view even when it shows several moments, repeated figures, a composited portrait or silhouette, or changes of scale. A difference in subject, scale, time or perspective alone does not create a separate view. Conversely, a distinct inset or adjacent region remains separate even when it repeats the same characters, colors or setting. Enumerate the actual views visible in the region, including any insets, and explain the visual evidence. Do not assume an existing segmentation is correct. Return continuous for one composition, distinct for multiple, or uncertain when the visual evidence cannot distinguish them.
