## Smallest candidate changes to investigate

The missing obligation is **affirmative whole-source scene completeness before an otherwise direct native acceptance**. Lowering a positive threshold cannot resolve this observed zero probability. Adding more checks of only the surviving crops likewise does not directly test the absent scene.

The smallest candidate change is a mandatory stronger global completeness check before ordinary candidates can be kept directly. Qualify its exact prompt/schema on unchanged P0, N1 and the still-unscored N2 before integration; a demonstrated omission or uncertainty must remain binding through later crop passes. Existing ownership checks and independent split/inventory failures must retain their authority. This option is a proposed experiment, not an established remedy.

A more independent alternative is to inventory source scenes before showing any proposed count, geometry or descriptions, cache that source evidence by exact input/configuration, then require every source scene to map to the candidate. Count equality alone is inadequate: a merge plus a false split can preserve counts. The current source-ownership audit sees the proposed list and descriptions, so invoking it on every page without new anti-anchoring controls would not establish candidate-independent completeness. Deterministic uncovered-ink/gutter evidence could provide an additional escalation signal, but must be tested against covers, continuous backgrounds and borderless layouts before becoming a completeness rule.

No quality source, prompt or routing change was made during this audit. This failed control is retained as a regression; it does not invalidate the personally reviewed accepted development crops or prove universal failure of any model.
